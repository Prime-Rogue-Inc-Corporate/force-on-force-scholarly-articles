### Note on the Herrero PDF
The file was supplied under the name of Herrero, Higgins, Cardoza, Hajduk & Smith (2011), "Fatal
attacks by American black bear on people: 1900–2009", *Journal of Wildlife Management*
75(3):596–603, from the academia.edu link above. Its contents are not that paper: all eleven
pages carry the footer `Ursus 14(1):44-54 (2003)` and the text is the 2003 Alberta study. Filed
and reviewed under its real identity. The 2011 paper is listed under **Wanted** below. A direct
link for the 2003 article has not been supplied, so none is recorded for it.
